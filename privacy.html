Privacy Policy

This is a demonstration website. The policy below is sample wording written to show how a contractor's privacy page is normally structured before publishing. Replace it with legally reviewed text for your business.

Demonstration notice. This site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or marketing pixels, and the enquiry form does not transmit or store any information. Everything is handled locally in your browser.

Information we would collect

If this were a live contractor website, the enquiry form would collect the name, company, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, project location, project type, approximate budget, project stage and any requirements you describe. This information is used only to respond to your enquiry, prepare an estimate and arrange a site visit.

How information is used

  • Responding to project enquiries
  • Preparing estimates, proposals and site visit arrangements
  • Keeping a record of enquiries and follow-up communication
  • Improving the website and published project content

WhatsApp and lead handling

Enquiries submitted through WhatsApp are processed under the WhatsApp Business privacy terms. On a live website, enquiries would typically flow into a CRM pipeline so that every lead has an owner, a stage and a next action.

Cookies and analytics

This demonstration sets no cookies and runs no analytics. A production deployment would typically use privacy-respecting, cookie-consent-based analytics, with essential cookies limited to keeping the enquiry form working.

Data retention and security

Enquiry records are retained for as long as needed to pursue a project relationship and to meet statutory record-keeping obligations, and are then securely deleted. Access is restricted to the staff members working on your enquiry.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Requests can be sent using the contact details published on the website.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the project team. Contact details shown on this demonstration website are placeholders and should be replaced with the registered business contact information.
